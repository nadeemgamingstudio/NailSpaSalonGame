🌸 Privacy overview

Nail Spa Salon Game is a delightful, creative manicure and nail art simulation where you design, paint, and style stunning nails. We respect your privacy and believe in complete transparency. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — because your trust is the foundation of our salon.

Our promise: No selling of personal data, full compliance with child protection laws (COPPA/GDPR-K), and a safe creative space for all ages.

💅 1. What data we collect

We collect minimal information to keep your salon experience smooth, show relevant ads, and improve the game:

  • 📱 Device & technical info — model, OS version, language, country, anonymous advertising ID (AAID / IDFA), IP address. Used for analytics and ad functionality.
  • 🎮 Game progress & preferences — unlocked nail art items, level/score, saved designs, sound settings. Stored locally and optionally via cloud if you sign in.
  • 📢 Ad interaction data — via Google AdMob. The platform may use identifiers to serve contextual or personalized ads (respecting child settings).
  • 💎 In-app purchases — if you buy premium polish packs or remove ads, Google Play Billing handles the transaction. We never see your payment details.

We never request real names, location (beyond IP-derived region), contacts, or files from your device.

💅 2. How we use your information

Your data helps us deliver a polished experience:

  • Save game progress: keep your nail collections, stars, and unlockables.
  • Analytics & crash reports — using Firebase Crashlytics to fix bugs and improve performance across different devices.
  • Display ads (optional rewarded videos for extra nail designs) via Google AdMob.
  • Purchase verification — grant virtual items after successful Google Play transactions.
We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

💅 3. Third‑party integrations

To provide core features, we rely on trusted partners:

  • Google AdMob – serves ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded). AdMob may collect device data and IP for ad relevance. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics – tracks anonymized usage and crash data. Helps us make Nail Spa Salon Game more stable and enjoyable.
  • Google Play Billing – processing all in-app purchases. We never handle payment credentials.

Each provider complies with GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA where applicable.

💅 4. Data retention & deletion

Your nail designs and level progress are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the game, local progress may be removed. No account is mandatory. For any data deletion request related to analytics identifiers, contact us at nadeemgamer180@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Advertising identifiers can be reset via your device settings (Android: Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID).

💅 5. Your ad choices & opt-out

We give you control over personalized advertising:

  • On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • For users under the age of consent (varies by region), only non-personalized contextual ads are shown (COPPA mode).
  • Rewarded video ads are always voluntary; you choose to watch them for in-game bonuses.
Child-safe design enabled

💅 6. Children’s privacy (COPPA / GDPR‑K)

Nail Spa Salon Game is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA).

  • We do not require any personal data (name/email) to play the game.
  • If the Play Store identifies a child account, personalized ads and certain analytics are disabled automatically.
  • Parents/guardians: if you believe your child has unintentionally provided any personal info (e.g., through customer support email), contact us immediately and we will delete it.

💅 7. Data security

We adopt industry standards: encrypted network connections (TLS), minimal data collection, and restricted access to internal systems. Although no system is 100% invulnerable, we continuously update our security practices to protect your personal information.

💅 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your region (EEA, UK, California, Brazil, India), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data (if any linked to advertising ID).
  • Request rectification or deletion of data.
  • Opt out of “sale” (we never sell).
  • Withdraw consent for analytics by resetting your advertising ID.

To exercise any right, email nadeemgamer180@gmail.com with subject “Privacy Request – Nail Spa Salon Game”. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

💅 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. If changes are significant, we will notify you via an in‑game notice or by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the game after the update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
